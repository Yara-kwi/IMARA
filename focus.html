Health

Healthier Women, Children and Communities

We support better health outcomes for women, children and underserved communities by connecting health professionals, researchers and young advocates who understand local realities.

Technology & AI

Responsible Technology for Greater Impact

We promote the responsible and inclusive use of digital technologies and AI, so that innovation reaches the communities that need it most — not just the connected few.
